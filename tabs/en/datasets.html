Datasets & Data Collection

As part of my research, I have collected and contributed to several datasets related to environmental monitoring, remote sensing, and peatland ecosystems. These datasets provide valuable resources for understanding climate change impacts and developing monitoring methodologies.

Published, citable datasets

Contrasting physiological responses of Sphagnum and vascular plants to a heatwave in a temperate peatland

Leaf-level chlorophyll a fluorescence (OJIP/JIP-test), chlorophyll content, daily meteorology, water-table depth and pore-water chemistry from nine plots at the Rzecin peatland across the 2024 growing season, including the 2024 European heatwave.

RepOD 2026 Abdelmajeed et al. DOI 10.18150/GDSYNE

Midday depression and legacy effects disrupt SIF-GPP coupling in northern peatlands during combined heat and drought stress

Sun-induced fluorescence (FloX, ~30-second resolution) with ecosystem GPP, 30-minute meteorology and water-table depth from the Rzecin peatland (26 June – 2 July 2019) — the basis for studying SIF-GPP coupling under combined heat and drought stress.

RepOD 2026 Abdelmajeed et al. DOI 10.18150/YXLKT4
